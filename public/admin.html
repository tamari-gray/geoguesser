    How to run it

    1. Add your photos below and put them in order.
    2. Click Open presenter screen, drag that window onto the big monitor and press ⛶ for fullscreen.
    3. Teams scan the QR code (or type the URL) and enter a team name.
    4. Run the whole game from the presenter screen: click the button at the top, or press Space, to start and move to the next round.

    Rounds end after the timer, or early once every team has locked in. A pin dropped but not submitted still counts. The closest team gets 1 point per round.
